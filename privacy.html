Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026. This policy explains how Geometry Dash Avenue and Avenue Guard handle information across the public website, Discord bot, staff portal, applications, level requests, support tools, and operational systems.

Scope

This policy covers the Geometry Dash Avenue website, Avenue Guard, the authenticated staff portal, staff applications, public recommendation pages, and bot-assisted activity in the GD Avenue Discord server. Discord also processes server and account activity under its own terms and privacy policy.

Public Website

Most pages are public and do not require an account. The Idea Generator runs in your browser and does not send its selections to GD Avenue. Public bot and level pages request approved service status, release information, recommendation details, and Geometry Dash metadata from Avenue Guard.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell browsing profiles. Netlify and other infrastructure providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and time for delivery, security, diagnostics, and abuse prevention.

Discord Sign-In And Sessions

The staff portal and application area use Discord OAuth with the identify scope. Discord supplies the signed-in user's account ID and basic profile identity. Avenue Guard then checks guild membership and current Discord roles server-side. The browser cannot assign its own role or capabilities.

Sign-in uses a short-lived, signed OAuth state cookie. Successful sign-in creates an opaque session cookie that is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Strict, plus a secure CSRF cookie used to protect changes. Staff sessions currently expire after eight hours, and permissions are refreshed against Discord so role removal can remove access. A Dev may preview the portal with another role's effective capabilities, but preview mode is read-only, server-enforced, and does not impersonate another user. The portal stores only interface preferences in browser local storage.

Information Avenue Guard Stores

Depending on the features you use, Avenue Guard may store:

  • Discord IDs, display names, role-derived permissions, and relevant channel, message, thread, or guild IDs.
  • Level request forms, wave and edit history, review results, reviewer records, validation snapshots, recommendation type, queue state, hidden-state history, and outreach outcomes.
  • Tickets, support forms, appeals, reports, bug reports, transcripts, attachments or links you submit, staff responses, and satisfaction feedback.
  • Activity totals, weekly rankings and streaks, reward status, anti-farming signals, and daily or weekly aggregate summaries.
  • Moderation and workflow events, forum enforcement records, command actions, errors, health measurements, delivery state, impact reports, and backup or restore records.
  • Staff portal profiles and nicknames, manually managed team records, applications, age brackets, availability, timezone answers, written responses, selected level-review prompts, internal notes, tasks, queue claims, quality checks, and security audit events.

A failed lookup is recorded as unavailable rather than silently treated as valid data. Present-day Geometry Dash snapshots are kept separate from historical facts where the system cannot reliably know what was true at the time of a request.

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate request waves and recommendations, provide support, process staff applications and interviews, notify staff about assigned tasks, enforce server rules, prevent abuse, calculate community activity rewards, authenticate staff, maintain reliable delivery, investigate errors, restore service data, and understand the community impact of Avenue Guard.

Our processing supports the community services you request and GD Avenue's legitimate interests in safety, moderation, service reliability, and improvement. Where consent or another legal basis is required, we use that basis. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the specific support or safety issue.

Staff Applications

Reviewer and Mod applications use configurable question types such as a selected age bracket, availability, timezone, short answers, long answers, moderation scenarios, and, for Reviewers, a written review of one randomly selected public YouTube showcase. The application page can suggest the timezone reported by your browser; it does not use a location service. Avenue Guard enforces one active application and a five-day cooldown after submission. Submitted answers are copied into a private Discord review thread for authorized staff. Staff may reject the application, accept it, place it on hold, or create a private Discord interview ticket that the applicant and authorized staff can access. Decision and delivery events are retained for accountability and retry safety.

Do not include precise birth dates, home addresses, school details, private credentials, or unrelated personal information. The age question asks only for a broad bracket.

Requests, Recommendations, And Automation

Request validation may retrieve current public Geometry Dash level and creator information. The priority system can help order approved recommendations for staff outreach, but it does not guarantee moderator contact, a Geometry Dash rating, or any outcome. Human reviewers choose the review result and recommendation type.

A public page at /level/[level-id] may show a recommended level's public Geometry Dash details, recommendation type, date, thumbnail, and non-sensitive queue status. It does not publish the requester ID, private reviewer notes, exact priority score or rank, creator-point details, private outreach targets, claims, or internal audit data.

Service Providers And Sharing

Geometry Dash Avenue does not sell personal information. Data is available only to authorized staff where their role requires it and to service providers that operate the system. These currently include Discord for identity and community features, Netlify for the website and serverless gateway, Render for Avenue Guard, and Turso/libSQL for durable database storage.

Level IDs and other public Geometry Dash identifiers may be sent to metadata providers used by the validation and thumbnail systems. External images may be served by Discord, ImgBB, or the configured level thumbnail service. Information may also be disclosed to Discord Trust and Safety, safety partners, or legal authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required. Each external provider processes data under its own policy.

Retention And Backups

Records are retained only while they remain useful for operations, safety, appeals, historical integrity, reporting, or legal obligations. Current automated retention settings keep health measurements for 365 days, workflow and error records for 730 days, delivery records for 90 days, cached Geometry Dash validation for 7 days, and impact snapshots for 1,095 days. Other core records, including reviews, applications, interview tickets, transcripts, moderation evidence, staff audits, and recommendation history, may be retained longer when continuity or accountability requires it. A Dev applicant can remove their own stale application database record through a confirmed reset; already-created private Discord threads or interview channels may remain for audit integrity. Hiding a level removes it from normal and public workflows but retains the reversible internal record and its audit history.

Turso is the durable source of truth. Encrypted transport, controlled database access, periodic backups, and restore checks reduce data-loss risk. Deletion from the live database may not immediately remove an item from limited backup copies, which expire or are replaced on their normal schedule.

Security

OAuth client secrets, the Discord bot token, Turso credentials, and internal service credentials remain server-side. The portal uses signed OAuth state, hashed session tokens, CSRF protection, server-side capability checks, live role refreshes, idempotency controls, private audit logs, and restricted API responses. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but access is designed around least privilege and revocation.

Your Choices And Rights

You may use public website pages without signing in. You can sign out of authenticated areas, clear local portal preferences in your browser, and avoid submitting optional form fields. You can also control your Discord account and server participation through Discord.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information associated with you. Requests may be limited where retaining information is necessary for security, abuse prevention, another person's rights, legal duties, or the integrity of moderation and review records. Contact GD Avenue staff through the Discord server and include enough information to identify the relevant record. For data controlled by Discord, use Discord's privacy tools or contact Discord directly.

Young Users

GD Avenue relies on Discord's age requirements and does not knowingly invite users who are not permitted to use Discord. Never submit your address, school, phone number, legal documents, private account credentials, or other unnecessary identifying information in public channels, requests, applications, or support forms.

Policy Changes And Contact

We update this policy when Avenue Guard, the staff portal, or our providers materially change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Questions, privacy requests, and concerns can be sent to GD Avenue staff through the GD Avenue Discord server.
